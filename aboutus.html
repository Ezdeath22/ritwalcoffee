The Ritwal Story

Ritwal Coffee began with a simple idea: to create a space where students and faculty could find a moment of peace and a great cup of coffee. Born within the vibrant community of the University of St. La Salle, we quickly grew from just a café into a cherished daily "Ritwal."

Our mission is to serve more than just coffee. We serve comfort, energy, and a reliable smile. We believe that the simple act of grabbing a coffee can be the start of something truly great—a productive study session, a new friendship, or a brilliant idea. We are proud to be a part of your daily life, providing affordable, high-quality, and locally-sourced brews with the speedy service you count on.
